Is ceramic coating worth it for a new car?

A new car’s paint can still have marks or imperfections, so it’s worth checking the finish before applying a coating. Proper preparation helps the coating bond to the paint and gives the finish a clean, glossy look.

Ceramic coating can make routine washing easier and help protect the paint from some environmental contaminants. It won’t prevent stone chips, deep scratches or dents. If you’re mainly concerned about impact damage, ask us whether paint protection film may suit your needs better.
