A thorough clean, inside and out

What’s included

The service combines exterior cleaning, machine polishing and interior care. We’ll confirm the scope for your vehicle before booking.

Exterior detail and cut & polish

  • Pre-rinse and hand wash
  • Remove bug marks and road tar where present
  • Clean wheels, tyres and wheel arches
  • Clean and dry door, boot and fuel-door jambs
  • Paint decontamination
  • Cut and polish to improve gloss and reduce the appearance of suitable marks
  • Dress exterior rubber and plastic trim
  • Clean windows and mirrors
  • Finish tyres, arches and mud flaps

Interior detail and steam clean

  • Wipe down interior surfaces
  • Vacuum seats, carpets, boot, floor mats and compartments
  • Shampoo and steam clean seats, carpets, boot and floor mats
  • Clean and condition leather seats where applicable
  • Clean dashboard, console, trims and plastic surfaces
  • Clean air vents, seat rails, sun visors and pedals
  • Clean interior windows and mirrors
  • Deodorise the interior

Common questions

Before you book

Does Shelby offer mobile full detailing in Melbourne?

Yes. Shelby comes to your home or workplace. Call Savvy on 0490 699 614 to check availability for your suburb and site setup.

Will cut and polish remove every scratch?

No. Cut and polish can reduce the appearance of suitable swirl marks and light scratches, but deeper scratches may remain. The paint should be assessed before work begins.

Is the interior steam cleaned?

The service includes shampooing and steam cleaning the seats, carpets, boot and floor mats. Leather seats are cleaned and conditioned where applicable.

How much does a full detail and cut & polish cost?

The draft guide lists $349 for a hatch or sedan, $399 for a small SUV or wagon and $449 for a large SUV, 4WD or 7-seater. These prices need confirmation before publishing. Final pricing may depend on vehicle size, condition and the work required.
