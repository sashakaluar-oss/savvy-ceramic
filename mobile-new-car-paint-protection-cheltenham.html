What We Notice About New Cars in Cheltenham

Cheltenham vehicles can face open parking, coastal air, tree residue and regular cross-suburb travel. Horizontal panels often show the first signs of poor washing or mineral spotting.

A new car parked outdoors most days needs realistic aftercare advice. Coating reduces maintenance effort; it does not make bird droppings or salt residue harmless.

Our Inspection-First Approach

We do not assume a low-kilometre car is coating-ready. Before recommending a package, we look at the paint under useful lighting, feel for bonded contamination, check high-touch areas and ask how the car will be parked and washed. That determines whether the vehicle needs decontamination only, a light enhancement, or more correction than the starting package allows.

On a new vehicle in Cheltenham, we commonly check for:

  • Mineral spots on roof and bonnet
  • Residue around mirrors
  • Tree sap
  • Light scratches from automatic washes

A Detailer’s Decision, Not a Product Shortcut

We coat now when: the paint is stable, clean, properly prepared and the work area supports correct application and curing.

We recommend extra preparation when: swirl marks, water spots or bonded contamination would remain visible under the coating.

We pause the job when: weather, access, fresh paint, unresolved damage or unsafe conditions could compromise the result.

What the $499 Starting Price Means

The starting price is for selected brand-new vehicles with paint that requires limited correction. It is not a blanket price for every size or condition. A larger SUV, dark paint with dealership marring, heavy contamination or a higher-specification coating package may cost more. We explain that before work begins.

How We Choose Between Gtechniq and Nasiol

We use professional products from ranges such as Gtechniq and Nasiol. We do not choose purely by the longest durability claim. We consider the paint, intended use, storage, maintenance habits and package budget. Preparation quality and aftercare usually have more influence on the real result than the logo on the bottle.

What We Tell Every New-Car Owner

Ceramic coating is useful because it can improve gloss, water behaviour, resistance to common contamination and ease of washing. It is not stone-chip protection, it does not prevent parking damage, and it is not permission to ignore bird droppings or use harsh automatic washes. For impact protection, paint protection film is the relevant category.
