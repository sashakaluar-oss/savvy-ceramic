Ceramic Coating Cost Breakdown — Australia 2025

Understanding ceramic coating pricing requires understanding what drives the cost. There are four components: product cost, surface preparation, application time, and business overheads. A $299 "ceramic coating" from a mobile detailer doing 10-minute spray applications is not the same product or process as a professionally prepared, multi-layer coating applied in a controlled environment.

What's Included at Each Price Point

Essential — From $499: Full hand wash, clay bar decontamination, IPA panel wipe-down, 1 layer of professional ceramic coating. Suited to new cars or vehicles in good condition. 2-year protection rating.

Premium — From $899: Everything in Essential, plus minor paint correction to remove light swirl marks and water spots, 2 layers of ceramic, glass coating included. 5-year protection rating. Our most popular package.

Ultimate — From $1,499: Multi-stage paint correction (removes deep swirls, scratches, oxidation), 3 layers of professional ceramic, wheels and plastic trim coated, glass coating. 7-year protection rating. Suited to prestige and performance vehicles.

What Makes Ceramic Coating More or Less Expensive?

  • Vehicle size: A large SUV or 4WD takes more product and time than a small hatchback. Expect $100–$200 more for larger vehicles.
  • Paint condition: A new car needs minimal prep. A 5-year-old daily driver may need significant paint correction. Correction adds $200–$600 to the job depending on severity.
  • Number of layers: Each additional layer adds cost in product, time, and curing. 3-layer jobs take 2 days vs 4–6 hours for single-layer.
  • Who's doing it: A specialist ceramic detailer charges more than a general detailer doing coating as an add-on — and rightly so. The quality difference is significant.

How Much Does 3M Ceramic Coating Cost in Australia?

3M ceramic coating products (such as 3M Performance Finish or 3M Ceramic Coat Plus) are typically dealer-supplied and range from $800–$2,500 at dealerships, where significant margin is built in. At independent specialist detailers, 3M products are priced on application time and prep — typically similar to other professional-grade ceramic products ($499–$1,499).

However, the brand name matters less than the application quality. A correctly applied Gyeon, Gtechniq, or Carpro Cquartz product will outperform a "3M" sticker applied over poorly prepped paint by an untrained applicator every single time.

How Much Should a 2-Year Ceramic Coating Cost?

A genuine 2-year professional ceramic coating should cost $350–$600 for a passenger vehicle. If you're quoted under $250 for a "ceramic coating," it is almost certainly a spray sealant or consumer-grade product with no professional preparation — not a true ceramic coat.

Is Dealer Paint Protection Worth the Cost?

Dealership paint protection packages in Australia are notoriously poor value. You're typically paying $800–$3,000 for a product that takes a dealer detailer 20–30 minutes to apply, with no paint correction, no decontamination, and often a product quality far below what professional detailers use. Decline the dealer package and book with a specialist instead.

Related Questions

Is a cheaper ceramic coating still worth it? +
If 'cheaper' means a professional with a lower-cost product but proper prep — yes. If it means a spray-and-go 20-minute application with no decontamination — no. The prep work is 70% of what determines how long the coating lasts.
Why do some detailers charge $200 for 'ceramic coating'? +
At that price point, the product is almost certainly a SiO2 spray sealant or consumer-grade ceramic spray — not a professional ceramic coating. These products sit on top of the paint and wash off in weeks, not years. Always ask what specific product is being applied and how long the prep process takes.
