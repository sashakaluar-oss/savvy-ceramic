What paint correction can do

Paint correction is machine polishing that can reduce the appearance of some defects, including light swirl marks and haze. The result depends on the paint’s condition, previous polishing and the correction process selected.

Cut and polish is a common name for machine polishing. It can describe a one-step polish or more involved correction, so the scope should be clear before work begins. Deeper scratches and stone chips may need a different repair. Polishing removes a small amount of clear coat, so not every mark should be chased.

When you are considering ceramic coating

Paint correction can be carried out before a ceramic coating when the paint needs it. A coating adds a protective layer, but it does not conceal existing scratches or make paint damage-proof. We can discuss the finish you want and whether correction is suitable before you choose a package.

Questions about polishing

Before you book

Is cut and polish the same as paint correction?

Cut and polish is a common term for machine polishing. The scope may be a light single-step polish or a more involved correction, depending on the paint and the result you want.

Can polishing remove every scratch?

No. Some marks are too deep or need a different repair. Polishing also removes a small amount of clear coat, so Shelby checks the paint and discusses what is realistic before work starts.

Can Shelby do the work at my property?

Shelby is mobile across Melbourne’s south-east. Mobile availability and the site setup are confirmed for each address before the booking is finalised.

What information should I send?

Share your suburb, vehicle make and model, the service you are considering and clear photos of the paint. This helps Savvy discuss the likely scope and prepare a quote.
