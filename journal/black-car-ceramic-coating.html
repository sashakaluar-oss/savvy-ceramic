Paint care · Shelby Journal

Is ceramic coating a good choice for a black car?

A ceramic coating can suit a black car, but it will not hide existing swirls or scratches. Paint condition and preparation should be discussed before the coating is applied.

Dark paint can make light marks and dust easier to notice. If the finish has visible swirls, ask whether paint correction should be included before coating.

After application, gentle washing and clean wash tools help reduce the chance of adding new marks. The coating still needs regular care.

Further reading: manufacturer guidance. Follow the instructions for the specific product applied to your vehicle.
