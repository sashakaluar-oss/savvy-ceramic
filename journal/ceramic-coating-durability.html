Package durability · Shelby Journal

How long does ceramic coating last?

Shelby’s current packages state two years of durability for Essential, five years for Premium and seven years for Ultimate.

These are package durability statements, not a promise that every vehicle will perform the same way for the full period. The coating product, paint preparation, application and aftercare all matter. Ask Savvy which product is included in your package and follow its care instructions.

Current package statements

  • Essential: Two-year stated durability
  • Premium: Five-year stated durability
  • Ultimate: Seven-year stated durability

Those durations are taken from Shelby’s current published package details. Check the product-specific aftercare guidance supplied for your vehicle.

What ceramic coating can and cannot do

A ceramic coating can add gloss and water repellency and can make routine washing easier. It does not make paint scratch-proof or prevent stone chips. If the paint has swirls or marks, ask whether paint correction should be discussed before coating.
