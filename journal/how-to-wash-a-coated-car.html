How should you wash a ceramic-coated car?

Wash a coated car with clean tools and follow the aftercare instructions supplied for the coating. Avoid dragging dirt across the paint and clean heavy contamination promptly.

Use a method and products that suit the coating manufacturer’s guidance. Keep wheel-cleaning tools separate from paint-washing tools and use a clean drying towel.

Aftercare varies between products. The installer should tell you when the first wash is safe and which products to avoid.

Further reading: manufacturer guidance. Follow the instructions for the specific product applied to your vehicle.
