Why does paint preparation matter before ceramic coating?

Paint preparation matters because a coating follows the surface it is applied to. Cleaning and suitable paint correction help prepare the finish before coating.

undefined

The process can include washing, decontamination and inspection. If the paint has swirls or marks, polishing may be discussed before application. The amount of correction depends on the vehicle and the result you want.

Ask what preparation is included in your package and whether extra correction is recommended. A clear quote should set out the work before it begins.

Advice can vary by vehicle and paint condition. Ask your detailer what applies to your car.
