When can you wash your car after ceramic coating?

The safe wait time depends on the coating product and application conditions. Follow the product-specific aftercare instructions from your installer.

There is no single wait time that applies to every coating. Temperature, moisture and the coating system can affect the curing advice.

Before the job, ask when the car can be washed, when it can get wet and what to do if bird droppings or other contamination lands on the paint. Keep those instructions with your vehicle records.

Further reading: manufacturer guidance. Follow the instructions for the specific product applied to your vehicle.
