Ceramic coating or car wax: what is the difference?

Wax and ceramic coating are different paint-care options. Wax is usually a shorter-term finish, while a professional ceramic coating is designed to bond to prepared paint and last longer.

The right choice depends on your budget, how you use the car and how much upkeep you want. A coating still needs regular washing and does not remove the need for good care.

Ask for the product name, expected durability, preparation steps and maintenance instructions. A quote should explain what is included rather than relying on a broad promise.
