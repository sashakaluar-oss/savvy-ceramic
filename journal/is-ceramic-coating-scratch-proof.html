Is ceramic coating scratch-proof?

No. Ceramic coating does not make car paint scratch-proof and it will not stop stone chips. Careful washing and realistic expectations still matter.

A coating can add a protective layer and may improve resistance to some everyday contaminants, but sharp impacts, abrasive washing and road debris can still mark paint.

If the paint already has swirls or scratches, ask whether paint correction is suitable before coating. Ceramic coating is not a filler for visible defects.

Further reading: manufacturer guidance. Follow the instructions for the specific product applied to your vehicle.
