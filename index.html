A straight answer

What does ceramic coating do?

Ceramic coating bonds to painted surfaces to add gloss and water repellency and can make routine washing easier. It does not make paint scratch-proof or stop stone chips.

Results depend on the product, preparation, paint condition and aftercare. Shelby can inspect your car and explain what a coating can realistically do.

Compare coating and wax

Does coating remove scratches?

No. Ceramic coating does not repair scratches. Paint correction may reduce some visible defects before application, depending on their depth and the paintwork.

Does a coated car still need washing?

Yes. A coating can make cleaning easier, but your car still needs regular, suitable washing and care.

Common questions

Is ceramic coating scratch-proof?

No. Ceramic coating is not scratch-proof and does not stop stone chips. It can improve gloss, water repellency and cleaning. Paint correction may address some existing marks before application, depending on their depth.

Does Shelby offer mobile ceramic coating?

Yes. Shelby offers mobile ceramic coating and paint protection at homes and workplaces across selected Melbourne suburbs. Include your suburb in the quote form or call 0490 699 614 to check availability.

How much does ceramic coating cost?

Shelby’s listed packages start from $499, $899 and $1,499. Exact price depends on vehicle size, paint condition and preparation needed. Ask for a quote for your car.

How long does ceramic coating last?

Durability depends on product, preparation, conditions and care. Shelby lists package durability figures of 2, 5 and 7 years. Ask which product and care requirements apply to your package.
