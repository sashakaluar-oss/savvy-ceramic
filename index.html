11 · Before you book

Common questions

Does ceramic coating make a car scratch-proof?

No. Ceramic coating does not make paint scratch-proof or prevent stone chips. It can make routine cleaning easier, but careful washing still matters.

Does Shelby come to my home or workplace?

Yes. Shelby is a mobile ceramic coating and car detailing service. Share your suburb and site setup when you request a quote so Savvy can confirm what will suit the job.

How long should I wait before washing after coating?

Aftercare and curing instructions depend on the coating product and conditions. Follow the instructions supplied for your vehicle and contact Shelby if you are unsure.

What should I include when asking for a quote?

Include the make, model, year, suburb, service you want and a few details about the paint condition. Photos can help Savvy understand what preparation may be needed.
