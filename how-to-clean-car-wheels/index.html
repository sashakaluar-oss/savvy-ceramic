How to clean your car wheels with just 3 things

Brake dust builds up quickly, and wheels can be awkward to clean, especially if they have lots of spokes or tight corners.

The good news is you don’t need a cupboard full of products. For a basic clean, you’ll need a suitable wheel cleaner, a wheel brush and a clean microfibre cloth. Add a tyre cleaner if you want to scrub the tyres as well.

In Melbourne, it’s worth choosing your moment. Clean the wheels when they’re cool and out of direct sun. On a hot summer day, a wet wheel can dry quickly and leave marks. In winter, a break in the rain makes the job a lot more pleasant.

What you’ll need

For the wheels:

  • A wheel cleaner that’s safe for your wheel finish
  • A soft wheel brush
  • A clean microfibre cloth for drying

For the tyres, add a separate tyre cleaner and tyre brush. Don’t use the same cloth on your wheels and paintwork.

Always check the product label and your vehicle or wheel manufacturer’s care advice first. If you’re unsure what finish your wheels have, test the cleaner on a small area before using it across the whole wheel.

How to clean your wheels

1. Let the wheels cool

Don’t spray cleaner onto hot wheels. If you’ve just driven, park in the shade and give them time to cool.

2. Rinse off loose dirt

Use water to rinse away loose grit. This helps reduce the chance of dragging dirt across the wheel while you clean.

3. Apply the wheel cleaner

Follow the product instructions and work on one wheel at a time. Don’t let the cleaner dry on the surface.

4. Brush the wheel

Use a soft brush to reach around the spokes, inner barrel and other tight spots. Take your time around delicate finishes and avoid using a brush that’s picked up grit.

5. Rinse and dry

Rinse the wheel thoroughly, then dry it with a clean microfibre cloth. This helps prevent water spots and leaves the finish looking tidier.

A light clean may take around five minutes per wheel. If there’s heavy brake dust or built-up grime, allow more time rather than rushing the job.

Want to clean your tyres too?

Use a tyre cleaner and a separate tyre brush. Tyres can hold grime that you don’t want to spread onto the freshly cleaned wheel. Rinse well and follow the product directions.

When should you clean your wheels in Melbourne?

A regular clean helps stop brake dust and road grime from building up. After wet weather or a long drive, check the wheels when you wash the car. In summer, work in the shade and clean them while they’re cool. In winter, choose a dry spell if you can.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to clean car wheels?

For a basic wheel clean, use a wheel cleaner that’s suitable for the finish, a soft wheel brush and a clean microfibre cloth. Add a tyre cleaner and separate tyre brush if you’re cleaning the tyres too.

Can I clean wheels when they’re hot?

It’s better to wait until they’ve cooled. Heat can cause the product to dry too quickly, so clean the wheels in the shade and follow the product instructions.

How often should I clean my wheels?

There’s no set schedule for every car. Check them when you wash your vehicle and clean them before brake dust and grime build up heavily.
