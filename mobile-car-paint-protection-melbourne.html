What mobile paint protection involves

Paint protection starts with the vehicle’s condition and the finish you want. Ceramic coating is applied after suitable preparation. The package and preparation depend on the size of your vehicle and the state of the paint.

A coating can add gloss, water repellency and make routine washing easier. It does not make paint scratch-proof or prevent stone chips. Careful washing and the product’s aftercare instructions still matter.

A few practical questions

Before booking mobile paint protection

Does a coating protect against every scratch?

No. Ceramic coating is not scratch-proof and does not prevent stone chips. It can add gloss and water repellency, and make routine cleaning easier.

Will Shelby travel to my suburb?
