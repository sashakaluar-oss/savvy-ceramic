Why Blackburn Cars Need Ceramic Coating

Blackburn's established deciduous trees produce sap, tannins, and organic acids that etch into unprotected clear coat — particularly in summer when Melbourne's UV accelerates the damage. Ceramic coating creates a chemical-resistant barrier that prevents these contaminants from bonding.
