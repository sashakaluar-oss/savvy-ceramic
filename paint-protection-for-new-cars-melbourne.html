Paint Protection for New Cars in Melbourne, The Complete Guide

Every week in Melbourne, a new car owner is standing in a dealership being offered paint protection. Some of those packages are good value. Many are not. Here's what I tell people who call me after picking up their new car, and before they've signed anything at the dealership.

Why New Cars Need Paint Protection in Melbourne

The misconception: new car paint is somehow tougher than older paint. The reality: clear coat is at its most vulnerable when it's fresh. Factory paint has minimal existing protection once the car leaves the production line, it relies on you to apply protection before Melbourne's environment starts working on it.

Melbourne's UV Index 10–12 in summer begins oxidising exposed clear coat within months of ownership on outdoor-parked vehicles. Melbourne's bird population doesn't care that your car is new. And the first time you wash a new car at a drive-through car wash, you're introducing swirl marks that ceramic coating would have prevented.
